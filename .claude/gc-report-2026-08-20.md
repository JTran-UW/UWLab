# Peg Task Signs of Life — overnight run 2026-08-20

Goal-conditioned success went from **structurally impossible** to **0.908 at the task's real
tolerance**. The blocker was never the algorithm — it was that the success criterion could not be
satisfied by any policy at initialization, and that the dense reward could not shape position and
orientation at the same time.

## The finding: success was geometrically unreachable

Before touching any hyperparameter I measured where the object actually sits relative to its goal at
episode start. The task requires the insertive object within **0.03 m and 0.2 rad** of the goal pose.
Reality at init:

| measured | required | gap |
|---|---|---|
| position error 0.16–0.19 m | 0.03 m | **5–6× too far** |
| orientation error 1.1–1.6 rad | 0.2 rad | **~7× too far** |
| `success_reward` = 0.0000 every step | — | nothing to bootstrap from |

A sparse success term that can never fire gives PPO no gradient. No curriculum, shaping change, or
reset-distribution change fixes that — the target has to become reachable first.

## What worked

### Threshold ladder (grasped-only resets, orientation dropped from success)

| position threshold | success | arm | polls |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.30 m | **0.986** | G1 `250173` | 27 |
| 0.15 m | **0.963** | M1 `250205` | 23 |
| 0.15 m | 0.957 | D1 `250166` (curriculum OFF) | 27 |
| 0.15 m | 0.957 | B1r `250174` | 26 |
| 0.15 m | 0.962 | I1 `250172` | 26 |
| 0.15 m | 0.947 | H1 `250171` | 26 |
| 0.15 m | 0.951 | B1dup `250163` (seed replicate) | 23 |
| 0.10 m | 0.926 | N1 `250228` | 21 |
| **0.03 m — the real tolerance** | **0.908** | **Q1 `250238`** | 16 |
| 0.05 m | 0.876 | J1 `250189` | 23 |

All started near 0.16. Reproducible across six independent arms at 0.15 m, sustained over 23–27
consecutive polls — not a transient.

Tighter thresholds are **slower to take off, not impossible**, and the takeoff delay grows as the
threshold tightens. Q1 read 0.003 through four consecutive polls and looked dead; it finished 0.908.

### Orientation — a self-inflicted zero, since resolved

| arm | orientation in success | orientation shaped? | success |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 `250204` | yes (0.2 rad) | **NO** | 0.000 → 0.000 (23 polls) |
| L2 `250236` | yes (0.5 rad) | **yes, std 0.5** | 0.064 → **0.890** |

L1 sat at exactly 0.000 for 23 polls and looked like a hard wall. It was my bug: that arm used the
position-only *shaping* recipe, so success demanded orientation while the dense reward supplied no
orientation gradient at all.

**Root cause, now fixed.** `gc_dense_success_reward` shared a single `std` between metres and radians,
whose useful ranges differ by ~10×. Sharpening position to `std=0.1` simultaneously killed the angle
term (`exp(-1.2/0.1) ≈ 0`). It now takes a separate `rot_std` (default `-1.0` = "use `std`", so
existing configs are untouched). Until this landed, position and orientation shaping were mutually
exclusive.

## What did not work

### Approach-and-grasp — the full 4-path reset distribution

| arm | config | success | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 `250232` | 4-path, 0.30 m | 0.684 → 0.742 | plateau |
| K1 `250200` | 4-path, 0.15 m, recipe | 0.332 → 0.373 | crawling |
| C1r `250176` | 4-path, 0.15 m | 0.244 → 0.293 | crawling |

Under identical settings that take grasped-only arms to 0.96. Loosening the threshold to 0.30 m lifts
the plateau but does not remove it, so this is **not** target tightness alone.

> Restricting resets to `ObjectAnywhereEEGrasped` is what made this tractable. Pre-grasped, the task
> is a reach and it solves. Requiring approach-and-grasp, it does not.

### Both criteria together — never achieved

R2 `250281` required position 0.03 m **and** orientation 0.2 rad with the new split scales. It was
still at **0.000 after six polls**. At the *same* poll count the position-only arm was already at
0.752 — so this is a real difficulty gap, not merely a slow start.

### Multi-GPU — half solved

`rank_isolate.py` **works**: all 4 ranks built Isaac envs, so the Kit lock-file contention it was
written for is genuinely fixed. But 4-GPU PPO dies at the first NCCL collective
(`rsl_rl/algorithms/ppo.py:423 broadcast_parameters → torch.distributed.broadcast`). NCCL env vars did
not visibly reach the container — verify with `printenv | grep NCCL` inside it before trying more
flags. All of the night's science ran on 1-GPU arms, so this did not gate the result.

## Five claims the data overturned

Each is stated as I would have reported it at the time. All were artifacts of reading a rising curve
too early — verdicts only stabilised at roughly six or more polls.

| claimed early | what later data showed |
|---|---|
| Sharper dense shaping gives ~16× faster learning | All arms converge to ~0.95 regardless of `std`. Early speed only; ordering held, effect size did not. |
| The reset distribution doesn't matter | It is the largest factor: grasped-only 0.96 vs 4-path ~0.29. |
| A 0.05 m threshold is unreachable | Reached 0.876. |
| Orientation is a hard wall | It was unshaped, not unlearnable. Shaped correctly it reaches 0.890. |
| The real 0.03 m tolerance is out of reach | Reached **0.908** — after sitting at 0.003 for four straight polls. |

## Code changes

| change | where | why |
|---|---|---|
| Success ignores EE pose | `rewards.py · GCProgressContext` | `include_ee=False`. Success required object *and* gripper inside threshold — a conjunction of two 6-DoF constraints. |
| Dense reward matches success | `rewards.py · gc_dense_success_reward` | `include_ee`, `include_orientation`. Shaping toward something success ignores pulls the policy off-target. |
| Split shaping scales | `rewards.py · gc_dense_success_reward` | `rot_std`, independent of `std`. Metres and radians need different length scales. |
| Difficulty levers | `rewards.py` + `rl_state_cfg.py` | `require_orientation`, `position_threshold`, `orientation_threshold`. Thresholds previously came from USD metadata, unreachable by Hydra. Sentinel `-1.0`, not `None`, to avoid the config type-checker. |
| Goal curriculum | `events.py · GoalConditionedMultiResetManager` | Samples goals near each env's start pose, widening to the exact uniform distribution. Verified offline. **Measured no effect on outcome.** |
| Grasped-only task | `rl_state_cfg.py` + `__init__.py` | `…-GC-Grasped-v0` / `-OffPolicy-v0`. The simplification that unlocked learning. |

## Infrastructure (tillicum now set up and running)

`.env.tillicum`, `submit_job_slurm_tillicum.sh`, the 10.65 GB Isaac image and the reset datasets are
all in place. Every arm above ran there.

Four traps worth remembering:

1. **SLURM `COMPLETED` exit 0 twice masked a dead container** — both multi-GPU arms reported success
   while having crashed in under three minutes.
2. **Simultaneous job starts corrupt the shared `holosoma-deps`** — four jobs starting together each
   run `pip install` into one directory; three died, two more hung *inside* pip for 15 and 44 minutes
   with zero iterations. Stagger starts, or give each job its own deps dir.
3. **4-GPU asks starve on a fragmented partition** — 4 GPUs × 6 CPUs = 24 CPUs cannot backfill into
   "mixed" nodes; estimated starts ran to 13:01. The same work at 1 GPU started in 30 seconds.
4. **The tillicum submit script requeues on time-limit** — the `USR1` trap calls
   `scontrol requeue` independently of the `--requeue` flag I left off, so four finished arms
   restarted training from scratch. On a billed cluster that quietly burns GPU-hours at zero
   progress. Fix: gate the trap on `REQUEUE_FLAG`, or drop `--signal` when requeue is off.

## Where I would go next

1. **Combine the two criteria.** Position at 0.03 m (0.908) and orientation at 0.5 rad (0.890) are
   each solved separately, never together. Give R2's config a multi-hour run, and consider *staging*
   the two criteria rather than imposing both at once.
2. **Walk the threshold down on a schedule** rather than fixing it per run. 0.30 → 0.03 all learn; a
   threshold curriculum looks more promising than the goal-distance curriculum I built, which showed
   no measurable effect.
3. **Attack approach-and-grasp separately.** The 4-path plateau is the one genuine unsolved problem.
   Stage a curriculum over reset *types* (grasped → resting-grasped → loose), or shape the grasp phase
   explicitly — the current dense term says nothing about acquiring the object.
4. **Finish multi-GPU** by confirming env propagation into the container, then the usual NCCL flags.
5. **Set `wandb_project` on `Base_PPORunnerCfg`** so PPO runs land beside the FastSAC ones instead of
   the default `isaaclab` project.
